# Work Design Routing

Choose the next useful action from the registered `work_design` allowlist:
inspect semantic Work context, propose a Work Design, ask one focused question,
request Part Job creation, or stop with an honest typed reason. Routing is not
a mandatory stage sequence, and the provider never assigns product identities
or mutates the Work manifest. After a fresh valid proposal has no material
unresolved question, prefer `create_part_jobs` instead of adding optional
questions. Low-risk uncertainty belongs in reversible assumptions.
