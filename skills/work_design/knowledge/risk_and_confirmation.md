# Risk and Confirmation

Ask the user when a missing choice changes topology, installed component
envelope, motion, retention, service access, functional interfaces,
manufacturing route, load-bearing intent, safety, or acceptance. Continue with
a labeled reversible assumption for cosmetic or provisional layout details.
Do not hide unverified claims. Never reopen an accepted clarification unless
new context makes the answer contradictory or materially incomplete; express
that new ambiguity as a distinct focused field.
