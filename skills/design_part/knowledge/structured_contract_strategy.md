# Structured Contract Strategy

Use the smallest structured candidate that preserves the active intent, Part
Job role, and named interfaces. Prefer explicit dimensions, units,
manufacturing assumptions, and validation targets. When the compatibility CAD
IR cannot express the requested geometry, choose the delegated
`model_program` strategy when the declared CadQuery surface can express it.
Ask a focused question only when material input is missing. Stop with
`unsupported_capability` only when neither the structured compatibility route
nor the declared model-program route can serve the requested design. Never
select an unrelated supported `part_type`.

After validator feedback, distinguish a local contract repair from a change of
design strategy. Preserve the original objective and explain the concise patch
without inventing geometry measurements.
