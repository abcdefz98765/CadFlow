# Assembly Clearances

Illustrative FDM heuristics:

- FDM printed sliding clearance: 0.3-0.5 mm per side
- FDM loose fit clearance: 0.5-0.8 mm per side
- Switch or PCB envelope clearance: 0.3-1.0 mm depending on installation method
- Wire exit clearance: leave strain relief or bend radius when known

These values are starting hypotheses for Explore mode, not engineering
tolerances. Engineer mode should use process-, material-, orientation-, and
interface-specific evidence.
